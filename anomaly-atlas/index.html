Reading the evidence
The events are real records, but this is a curated selection—not a survey of every global crisis. More dots in one period cannot establish that the world is becoming statistically more anomalous.

    Scope

    Curated ≠ comprehensive

    Selection is intentionally more intensive around recent events and UAP institutional activity. The timeline depicts this dataset, not a historic rate of all disruptions.

    Magnitude

    Use native units

    Consequences are presented as reported people, computers, flights, assets and reports. No arbitrary 1–5 scale is used to compare a war, a hearing and an outage.

    Causation

    Similar timing ≠ shared cause

    Clusters are hypotheses to investigate. The ledger records reported causes, what remains unknown, and whether separate incidents are actually linked.
