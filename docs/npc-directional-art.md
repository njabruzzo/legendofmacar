# Directional companion art — 2026-10-07

Pordoom, Fendur, Orbo, Talpor and Noz have five painted views (S, SE, E, NE, N). SW, W and NW mirror SE, E and NE. Each view has idle, two alternating walk contacts, windup, contact/fire and recovery. Four dwarf ghost forms share the same geometry and use the existing stable cyan ghost renderer. Dead, crushed, sleeping and tied artwork keeps its existing path.

Macar retains the established shared body assets for maul, axe and crossbow. Carried grip anchors follow each gait frame; the side carry places the melee head behind the shoulder. A small occlusion mask restores fingers over the shaft. The side recovery/ranged hand anchor was corrected. The existing measured shaft lengths are unchanged.

The generated NPC sheets do not use an exact regular cell grid. NpcDirectionalAtlas identifies 30 separate full-body alpha components, orders five rows of six poses, and plants each on a shared 512px canvas. The source is decoded once per actor and the 30 composed poses are shared between living and ghost bindings. World loading waits for these poses before entering play.

Original sprite assets remain in place. These are generated directional variants referenced to each existing character, not the missing signed KEEP pack in PR #152. Other enemy families retain their existing artwork. Two-frame NPC walking is less smooth than Macar's four-frame gait. Mirrored facings preserve existing handedness mirroring rules. Small painted export fringes and perspective differences may remain; the browser matrix checks clipping and actual frame selection, not artistic equivalence to absent approved files.

## Generation references and accepted outputs

Built-in imagegen skill, transparent output, existing character sheets as references. Clipped Pordoom, Talpor and Noz candidates were rejected. Fendur's south aim/fire poses were repaired to point toward the viewer. Raw source PNGs are retained; no external image editing was used.
