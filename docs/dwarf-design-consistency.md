# Dwarf design consistency — 2026-10-07

Pordoom, Fendur, Orbo and Talpor now use version 2 directional sheets. Macar's established artwork is the style reference: broad adult dwarf build, shaggy fur mantle, textured dark leather, brass fittings, geometric studs and heavy fur-cuffed armored boots. Each NPC keeps its own face, hair, class colors and equipment. Living idle/walking/combat and the four cyan ghost forms share these new designs. Existing corpse/special-state art remains on its original rendering path.

All four sets retain five painted views and the established mirrored west directions, with six poses per view. The first generation pass had touching poses or edge clipping and was rejected. The accepted layout pass places thirty complete figures apart with transparent margins. Original generated files and prior v1 sheets are retained.

Dwarf stature is measured in the central body column above the planted boots, instead of including the highest staff ornament. This prevents Talpor's staff from shrinking his figure relative to Macar. Gnome art retains its existing scale rule.

Validation: 133 test files pass. NPC browser regression: 2,626 checks across 480 states, zero failures. Side-by-side Macar/dwarf browser comparison: 525 checks, zero failures. Automated checks establish selection, mirroring, separate full poses, clipping and scale mechanics; artistic consistency was reviewed visually in the comparison preview.
