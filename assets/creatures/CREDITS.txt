Legend of Macar — creature sprite credits
==========================================

Thin One walk timing and contact (PAINT v4f, bound in game)
  Base: Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) universal walk cycle
  Use: timing and foot contact for the humanoid / boss walk
        (w1, pass_a, w2, pass_b). The pixels are painted over that skeleton.
  License: CC-BY-SA 3.0 and GPL 3.0
  Credit: LPC contributors, Liberated Pixel Cup
  https://lpc.opengameart.org/
  https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets

The painted Thin One sheets themselves are original game art. The LPC cycle
is the motion base only.
